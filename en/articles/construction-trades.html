Skilled Trades

Construction and Skilled Trades Jobs in Europe

6 min readUpdated 2026

Skilled trades face some of the sharpest labor shortages in Western Europe, and unlike many white-collar sectors, formal certification often matters more here than a university degree.

Skilled Trades

Where the Shortage Is Most Severe

Germany, the Netherlands, and Belgium report persistent shortages of electricians, welders, plumbers, and construction workers, driven partly by aging local workforces and a shrinking number of young people entering trade apprenticeships domestically.

Certification: What Actually Transfers

Trade qualifications don't automatically transfer between countries the way some professional degrees can. Most destination countries require either a formal skills-assessment exam or a supervised trial period to confirm your competency matches local safety and building standards — electrical and gas-related trades tend to have the strictest requirements given safety implications.

Tip: Some countries offer an accelerated "recognition of prior experience" pathway for trades, weighing years of documented work experience alongside formal certificates — ask specifically about this rather than assuming you need to fully requalify.

Language Requirements Compare Differently Than Office Jobs

On-site trade work often requires less formal language proficiency than office roles for day-to-day tasks, but safety training and certification exams are typically only offered in the local language — meaning you'll need working proficiency for the qualification process itself, even if daily site communication is more forgiving.

Practical Steps to Get Started

  1. Check whether your trade is on the country's official shortage-occupation list — this often simplifies the permit process significantly.
  2. Contact the relevant trade chamber (e.g., Germany's Handwerkskammer) directly to ask about recognition of your specific certification.
  3. Look for companies that have sponsored foreign trade workers before — they typically have the process down and can guide you through it faster.
