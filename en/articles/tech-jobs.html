IT and Tech Jobs in Europe: Where the Demand Really Is

7 min readUpdated 2026

Tech is one of the few sectors in Europe where English-only roles are genuinely common, and where several countries actively compete for the same pool of international developers.

Tech

Why Berlin, Amsterdam, and Warsaw Compete for Developers

Berlin offers a large, mature startup scene and comparatively affordable living for a capital city. Amsterdam has one of the highest concentrations of English-speaking international tech companies in Europe. Warsaw and other Central European hubs offer lower costs of living alongside a fast-growing IT outsourcing and product-development scene — often with faster visa processing than Western Europe.

Remote-Friendly Hiring in Tech

Tech is unusually flexible about hybrid arrangements: many companies will sponsor a work visa for candidates who only need to be in the office a few days a month, or who work fully remotely for a European legal entity. This is far less common in other sectors, and worth asking about directly during interviews.

Tip: Search specifically for "visa sponsorship" alongside your role and target country — many job boards let you filter for this directly, saving you from applying to companies that won't sponsor.

Do You Need Local Language at All?

In product and engineering roles at international companies, often not — many teams operate fully in English. However, local language becomes more relevant for client-facing roles, government-adjacent tech (public sector IT contracts), and smaller local companies outside major hubs, where English proficiency is less universal.

Practical Steps to Get Started

  1. Build a portfolio or GitHub profile that speaks for itself — European tech recruiters often weigh this more heavily than a formal CV.
  2. Target company size deliberately: larger international companies sponsor visas more predictably than early-stage startups.
  3. Check each target country's specific tech/skilled-worker visa track, since several (Germany's Blue Card, France's Talent Passport) have faster processing for verified tech roles.
